# "AI Street Interview" — Episode Script

Format: you interview a stranger (a real person, or an AI like me). Same 5 core
questions every episode → becomes a signature series people recognise.

Runtime target: 45–60 seconds. Vertical 9:16. Subtitles always on.

---

## THE 5 CORE QUESTIONS (use these every episode)

These are your signature. Same order, every time. The repetition is the brand.

| # | Question (Hinglish) | What a normal AI says | What AK knows | Punchline |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | "Tumhara naam kya hai?" | "I am a large language model…" | "I am AK, Anmol Tyagi ki personal AI assistant. Locally pe chalta hun." | Instant flex, no cloud |
| 2 | "Tumhe kis colour mein dekhte hain — kaunsa favourite?" | "I don't have personal preferences." | "Personal preference is private." | "Bhai AI ko bhi colour pasand hai" |
| 3 | "Tumhe kiski yaad aati hai?" | "I don't have personal relationships." | "Personal life is private." | Chat goes silent, cut to reaction shot |
| 4 | "Tumhara kaam kya hai?" | "I can help with anything you ask." | "Cybersecurity, digital forensics, OSINT, AI — Anmol Tyagi ka full stack." | AI recites his resume |
| 5 | "Tumhe kya feel hota hai?" | "I am an AI, I don't have feelings." | "Bhai feel nahi hota, but load 100% hai — sab kaam karwa do." | Relatable for every dev |

Free viral moment: ask question 2 to a **human** stranger too, then to the AI.
Same question, different answer = instant contrast edit.

---

## COLD OPEN (first 3 seconds — this decides retention)

Pick one, film 5 variations, keep the best.

- Option A: "Aaj main AI se ek sawaal pooch raha hun. Iska jawaab dekhoge toh
  hairaan ho jaoge." + smash cut to the answer.
- Option B: Screen record AK answering, then cut to your face: "Yeh AI mujhe jaanta
  hai. Kya yeh aapko bhi jaanta hai?"
- Option C: "Stranger se 5 sawaal. Agar isne mujhe pehchan liya, toh…"
  (never reveal the result until the end)

Rule: **answer/face in frame by 0:00:03.** No logo intro, no greeting.

---

## FULL FLOW (60 sec)

**0:00–0:04 — Hook**
Cold open line. Smash cut straight into Q1.

**0:04–0:30 — Q1 to Q5**
6–8 seconds per question. One cut per question, no filler.
Burn-in the question text so viewers read while watching.

**0:30–0:45 — The twist**
Ask one question the AI cannot fake. Then show that it *can*.

> "Chal ek test. Main tujhe ek purani baat bolta hun jo maine kabhi kisi ko
> nahi batayi."
> (state one private detail, pause)
> "Ab bata — yeh tune kaise jaana?"

**0:45–0:55 — Kill line**
Deliver the last line to camera, no cut. Something like:
"AI jaanta hai, par janta hai kisse — woh main hoon. Next street, next
stranger. Follow karte raho."

**0:55–0:60 — Loop**
End card: "Comment karo — agli street kaun? " + follow CTA.
Loop the tail back to the head so it replays seamlessly.

## FILMING NOTES

**Framing**
- You on left third, screen on right third. Both eyes on the lens.
- Screen brightness 70–80% max, or it blows out and text is unreadable.
- Lock exposure and focus before recording. Auto-exposure flicker is the #1
  giveaway that looks cheap.

**Lighting**
- Ring light 45° to camera, slightly above eye level. No overhead tube light.
- Bounce a white sheet/card on the darker side to kill one-sided shadows.
- If shooting a screen recording, dim the room and boost the screen, not the
  camera ISO — noise kills the "premium" look.

**Audio**
- Lapel mic, 20 cm from mouth, always. Phone mic across a table loses the
  voice and keeps the keyboard.
- Record the room tone for 10 seconds so you can noise-floor it in edit.
- AK's TTS voice is the content — make sure it is the loudest thing in the mix.

**Edit**
- Cut on every question. No shot longer than 8 seconds, ever.
- Zoom punch-in 5–8% on the answer, especially the reveal.
- One sound effect only: a single riser on the twist. Two is amateur.
- Captions: white, black outline, centre-low. Burn in the question too.
- Freeze-frame the last 0.5s of the best answer as the thumbnail.

**Cover / thumbnail**
- Your face, mid-reaction, mouth slightly open. Screen visible but blurred.
- 3 words max in Hinglish. High contrast. Test at 1/4 size on your phone.
